THE WASTELAND REMEMBERS
A Meitou mod for Kenshi. Vanilla-plus: no new races, no super weapons, no rewritten factions.
Just a world that notices what you do, and remembers it.

Every feature below can be switched off in game (press U > Settings, or Esc > Settings > Mods),
and one slider sets how much of the added talk you hear, from all of it down to none.
"Record only" turns everything off except the squad record.


YOUR SQUAD'S STORY
------------------
- Squad record window (press U, or the button at the top right)
    Per character: kills (and of which factions), knockouts, leaders and named characters killed,
    limbs cut off and lost, damage dealt and taken, times downed, fights won and last stands,
    distance walked / ridden / sailed, food eaten, items crafted, hours spent on every kind of work.
- 75 titles earned from real deeds
    Member titles (the Hard Hand, Bonepicker, Field Medic, the Headsman, the Walker...), squad titles
    (the Grave-Fillers, the Ash Tide, the Gatekeepers...) and named kills (kill Holy Lord Phoenix and
    you become Narko). A higher rung quiets the lower one.
- Factions react to your titles by their own nature: some admire, some fear, some despise you and
  will not hire you. An admiring barman may give you something on the house.
- History: your squad's story, newest first. The Fallen: who died, where, who killed them, whether
  they were avenged, and an epitaph. "Save as text" writes it all to a file.
- Notices: a small message floats up when someone gains a skill level, and when a faction's standing
  with you rises (green) or falls (red).


A SQUAD THAT FEELS IT
---------------------
- Lookouts: when hostiles come near, your people call them out by name ("Dust Bandits on the ridge!")
  and a squadmate answers. An army on the march gets its own warning.
- Memorials: your people mourn the fallen by name, remember where they fell, and the town that took
  their arm.
- Grief: for three days after a death, the banter turns quiet, and wins turn angry, using the fallen
  squadmate's name ("That one was for Beep.").
- Victory lines after a won fight.


A WORLD THAT TALKS
------------------
- Thousands of new lines for townsfolk, bosses and factions vanilla barely voices, checked against
  the lore. Nomads with their own voice; cannibals who speak the way the game's own cannibals do.
- Bar talk: patrons talk among themselves, and once you are famous they tell tales about your squad.
- Rumours of what actually happened: who you killed, which outlaw took a beating, which town is at war.
- Town criers: now and then a local calls out the real news of your world (wars, marches, outlaws
  raiding, raids beaten off, big deaths), or their faction's proclamation. Never more than every
  few minutes, each piece of news once a day.
- Alms and offerings: some townsfolk ask for alms (Empire), an offering for Okran (Holy Nation) or a
  donation to the Anti-Slavers. Giving warms their faction toward you (up to friendly, never allied),
  and a generous squad earns a title for it.


THE ATLAS
---------
- A tab of the places you know (79) and the people talked about (80), filled by the gossip your squad
  really overhears. Hidden bosses stay a blur until the rumours add up.
- Who holds each town now, where you last saw someone.
- Every faction you have met: how it stands with you, its wars, which of your titles it admires,
  fears or despises, and its own title for you (Empire: Obedient ... General, or Trouble-Maker ...
  Enemy of the State; Holy Nation: Approved Trader ... Hero of Light, or Heathen ... The Devil).
- Meitou 0.19+: each person's page shows their face.


RIVALS
------
- Win a fight and leave someone alive, and they may come back for you. The survivor (the actual person
  your squad beat) is renamed for what happened: Hollin One-Arm, Tesk Crutch, Mara the Unbowed. Once you've
  walked away, word reaches you that they crawled off and swore to come back.
- A day or two later they hunt your squad across the map, a little stronger each time. One who lost a leg
  comes back on an iron leg. Beat them and they may get away again; lose, and they boast about it in every
  bar. Kill them or put them in chains to end it.
- Townsfolk near a rival talk about them by name, the Atlas keeps their story, and History and Recap note
  every turn. With no rival about, your next won fight that leaves someone notable alive always makes one;
  with one about, now and then more, at most three at a time.


OUTLAWS AND WORK
----------------
- 36 named outlaws, each with a home ground, contracts on their heads, and news about what they
  are up to (raiding, lying low, gathering men, licking wounds). News of an outlaw stays near
  their ground (5 km by default), and talk about a boss stays near their home.
- Successors: a few days after a named outlaw dies or is jailed, one of their gang takes over the
  same ground under a new name, with a smaller bounty, a new contract and their own gossip.
- Bounties that grow while an outlaw keeps raiding; contracts that pay more the further you travel.
- Every contract you finish makes the faction that posted it think a little better of you
  (up to friendly, never allied: they won't die for you).
- Quests and errands from townsfolk, with a sensible limit on open jobs.
- Town heat: towns remember your crimes, guards warn you, it fades with time. Push it too far and
  a town may hire bounty hunters. They follow you across the map and attack; beat them and one
  carries a warrant with your squad's name on it. Hurt a named outlaw's people and they may send
  men after you too.
- Trophies and keepsakes worn in a new neck slot: an outlaw's token, a fallen friend's keepsake,
  a faction's charm.
- Optional, off by default (still being tested): moving outlaw camps, harassment raids (small
  factions harass big ones; nothing changes hands).


SQUAD ROLES (Meitou 0.19+)
--------------------------
Give any of your squads a role from the game's own NPC routines, in the Squads tab:
  Guard home / Guard home day and night / Patrol nearby (defend only) /
  Rest at home / Wander from town to town / Escort another of your squads (like hired bodyguards).
Give it a home building where it sleeps. Your orders always come first; the squad picks its role
up again afterwards. Saved with your game.


MADE TO FIT OTHER MEITOU MODS
-----------------------------
- Recap: titles, last stands, avenged squadmates, broken marches and more appear in its
  "Previously on your adventure".
- Kenshi Ultimate Overhaul: its armies march for real; your lookouts know an army from a patrol,
  and a march you break goes into your History. Our small raids step aside, so nothing doubles up.
- Guild & Quests: guild ranks, seals and jobs go into your History and earn squad titles.
- Raid Commander: raids on your base you beat off are remembered; three and you are the Gatekeepers.
- Execution / Brutality: every finishing blow and severed limb counts for whoever did it.
- Meitou Transport: rides and voyages count apart from walking, with their own titles.
- Quest Log & Town Map: finds our outlaws and their bounties by itself.
- Landmark Banners: the Atlas lists each visited town's bar, doctor, police station and shops.
- TimeLight Map: both sit top right by default; move the map (topcam corner bottom-right) or our button.


REQUIREMENTS
------------
- MeitouClient 0.17.3 or later (0.19 recommended: faces and squad roles need it).
- One mod: LivingWasteland (the code and the data in one folder), below your other mods.
- A new game is recommended; in an existing save your record starts from zero.
