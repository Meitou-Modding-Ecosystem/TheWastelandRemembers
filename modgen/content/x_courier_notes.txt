x_courier: six courier errands (mod LivingWastelandHeat). Playtest notes.

NEEDS: BFrizz's Extra Extensions (the recipient takes the item). The handover uses the vanilla line reference
'give item', which modgen/content.py does not yet write: a patch to content.py is needed
(it adds `give_items` to refs_from and the offer's accepted line).
Without the patch the pack still builds but the giver hands over nothing and the errand cannot be finished.

COMMON SHAPE. Offer chance is 12 percent per talk, only when the giver likes you (relation above 5) and you have not
taken the errand. Accept: the giver speaks the 'accepted' line and the object appears in your pack. Carry it to the
named kind of NPC and talk to them: they speak first (only if you carry the object), you answer, they take the object
and react. Go back to ANY giver of the same kind and talk: pays the reward. No kills, no spawns, no world states.
Extra lines: while the errand is open a giver may nag (var 1); once finished, givers, recipients and bar patrons
mention it (var 99). Each item has a tiny value and is readable in the inventory tooltip.
Lost item or dead recipient: the errand stalls (recipient talk lines need the object). Not fixable in data.
Overlap: dissent_errands.json (de_*) and hn_women.json (hw_q_*) already hold word-only versions of the same ideas;
these add real objects and different pairings.

1. cr_cord  Knotted Cord, pays 600.
   Giver: a Holy Nation woman (Civilian, Shopkeep, farm peasant). Recipient: Anti-Slavers ninja (Anti Slaver Ninja
   or HQ), relation to Anti-Slavers +3. Check: cord appears; ninja says who tied the knots; woman pays and asks you
   not to come back.
2. cr_tally  Ronin's Day Tally, pays 500.
   Giver: a hive ronin (Hive Prince race, ronin packages). Recipient: a Southern or Western Hive worker or soldier
   drone. Check: the drone says the hive holds the count; the ronin pays and thanks you.
3. cr_hide  Scraped Hide Strip, pays 700.
   Giver: a Shek mercenary, bounty hunter, drifter, nomad, scavenger or outlaw (race Shek; not Kingdom). Recipient:
   Kingdom Shek shopkeeper or retainer, Shek Kingdom relation +2. Check: they react to the scraped mark, the giver pays.
4. cr_page  Folded Page, pays 800.
   Giver: any Tech Hunter. Recipient: a skeleton (town resident, barman, junkyard, patron). The skeleton reads the page
   (one of four short readings, random) and keeps it. Giver pays and does not ask what it said.
5. cr_coin  Dented Coin, pays 900.
   Giver: a Slave Traders NPC (guilty conscience). Recipient: Flotsam Ninja, relation +2. They take it, not forgiving.
6. cr_note  Sealed Note, pays 400.
   Giver: Empire farmer or bar patron. Recipient: any Mongrel. Relation +2. A note from home, passed on without a name.

Bar rumours: 5 per errand, 3 always, 2 only after the errand is done.
Report in the test: item appears on accept (the key check), taken on delivery, cats on report, no repeats of the offer.
