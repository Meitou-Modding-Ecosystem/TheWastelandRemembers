LivingWastelandLua: Lua scripts for KenshiLua (needs RE_Kenshi and KenshiLua).

PROBES (always on, change nothing): scripts/init/00..40. Console (Shift+Ctrl+L): LW.probe(), LW.persist_set(7)/LW.persist_get(), LW.spawn_probe(faction, squad).
FEATURES (all OFF; draft): scripts/lw/*.lua, switched on in scripts/lw/config.lua under `enabled`:
  notoriety  earned titles for significant bosses; grows with the total score. LW.title() gives the current one.
  heat       crimes witnessed by a faction raise heat against you; decays; tiers cold / hostile / hunted.
  hunters    (needs heat) at the hostile tier a faction occasionally sends a squad, out of sight, aimed at your leader.
  nemesis    a named enemy who knocks one of your people out is remembered, gets tougher, and may turn up near where you last met.
  outlaws    keeps one of the 36 named outlaws out in the open every few days (cooldown, chance), remembers kills.
  drops      gives Rhinobot's CPU and (25%) a cannibal's arm to your leader when you landed hits on the kill (the quest items we assumed drop).
  captives   LW.captive.carried(c) and a dialogue script (scripts/dialogue/lw_captive_comment.lua): faction-aware comments on a carried captive.
  base       a visitor in a player town who sees your squad comments from what the town has (shop, turret, wall, bed, cage). Best effort.
  recruits   the 12 recruits: a joining message, a loyalty number, they leave if knocked out too often.
  domepapers puts the Ashlands papers on the dome heads' bodies once (also still placed by the data lists; pick one if both prove to work).
  chatter    squad chatter rules: arrival/status barks are vetoed for a squad member with nobody of the squad within 350 units, and capped at 5 per in-game hour. Replaces hand-tuning chances.
  calm       OFF by default (try it only after the game has loaded and you have a save). No NPC sighting barks (EV_I_SEE_NEUTRAL_SQUAD / ALLY) for 25 real seconds after a game starts or loads, then at most 2 per 4 seconds, so the whole town does not talk to you the moment your squad appears. It installs its hook only once the world is running. Tune calmGraceSeconds, calmBurst, calmWindowSeconds in config.lua.
  (chatter option) ambientBurst: set to 2 or 3 (with chatter on) and when a whole squad of NPCs sees you at once only that many of them bark. Default 0 = off.
  LW.report()  runs the probes and writes everything to lw_log.txt in one block. Send that file.
  ideology   NPCs of the right factions (Holy Nation, United Cities, Shek, Anti-Slavers, Slave Traders, Tech Hunters, Flotsam, hives, skeletons, Dust Bandits, cannibals) occasionally remark on the ideology you declare. In the console: LW.ideology.list() shows the ids (same as the Kenshi Faction System: focus such as "okran", policies such as "slavery"); LW.ideology.set("okran", {"slavery","free_trade"}, "Name"); LW.ideology.clear(). Untested in game.
  body       squad members speak when they lose a limb, are bleeding badly or cannot walk, in a voice that fits them (stoic, dry, anxious, blunt, fixed per name). Reads the health data directly; untested in game. Try LW.body.poll() in the console.
  tracker    quest journal: tells you when a quest or contract changes (Journal updated / complete) and shows active ones in a window (Y).
             LW.tracker.report() prints active quests to the log; LW.tracker.debug() checks whether the variables can be read (LW.probe_vars() does too).
Data (outlaws, recruits, dome papers) comes from scripts/lw/data.lua, generated by modgen/gen_lua_data.py from the content packs.
The logic is tested against a mock of the engine (tests/lw_mock_test.lua and lw_mock_test2.lua, run with lupa); real behaviour needs a play test.
Saved numbers live in your characters' appearance data (the same way the Animal_Husbandry-Lua example saves).
