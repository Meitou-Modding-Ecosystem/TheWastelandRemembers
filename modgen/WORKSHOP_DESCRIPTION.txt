[h1]The Wasteland Remembers[/h1]
[i]Towns talk, outlaws react, your squad keeps its own story.[/i]

[b]Vanilla-plus[/b]: the Kenshi you know, with a world that notices you. No super weapons, no new races, no rewritten factions. More life, more memory and more consequences, written to fit the lore.

[b]Nearly everything can be switched off.[/b] Press [b]U[/b] for the Squad record window and open [b]Settings[/b]: every feature has a switch (search by typing, hover for what it does), one slider sets how much of the new talk you hear, and [b]Record only[/b] turns everything off except your squad's record. (Also under Esc > Settings > Mods.)

[b]Early version:[/b] play tested, by few people so far. Feedback and bug reports are very welcome (see the end).

[h2]Your squad's story[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Squad record:[/b] each character's kills by faction, knockouts, limbs taken and lost, fights won, last stands, distance walked, ridden and sailed, and time spent on every kind of work.
[*][b]Titles[/b] earned from real deeds (the Hard Hand, Bonepicker, the Headsman, the Walker...) and named kills: kill the Holy Lord Phoenix and you become [b]Narko[/b]. Factions react to your titles by their own nature.
[*][b]History[/b] and [b]the Fallen[/b]: who died, where, who killed them, whether they were avenged, an epitaph each. Save it all as text.
[*][b]Keepsakes:[/b] when someone falls, a squadmate keeps "Keepsake of <name>", which tells their story. Right-click it and the holder says a line for them.
[*][b]Notices[/b] for skill levels and for a faction's standing with you rising or falling.
[/list]

[h2]A world that talks[/h2]
[list]
[*]Thousands of new lines, checked against the lore, for townsfolk, bosses and factions vanilla barely voices.
[*][b]Bar talk[/b], [b]town criers[/b] calling out the real news of your world, and [b]rumours[/b] about what actually happened.
[*][b]Lookouts[/b] call out hostiles by name; a squad that grieves for days after a death.
[*][b]The Atlas:[/b] places and people you've heard of, filled by gossip you really overhear, with faces (Meitou 0.19+) and how every faction you've met stands with you.
[*][b]Ask for news:[/b] right-click someone in a town (Meitou 0.20.2+) and they tell you the biggest news that has reached them.
[*][b]News from other mods:[/b] when another mod reports a town falling or an upset in the arena, people near it talk about it.
[/list]

[h2]Rivals and pursuers[/h2]
Win a fight and leave someone alive, and they may come back: renamed for what you did ([i]Hollin One-Arm[/i]), stronger each time, talked about in town, listed in the Atlas, and with something to say when they find you. Lose to one and they'll boast about it.

[h2]Outlaws, crime and work[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]36 named outlaws[/b] with contracts and bounties that grow while they raid. When one falls, someone from their gang takes over under a new name. Hurt their people and they lie low, lick their wounds, or send men after you.
[*][b]Town heat:[/b] towns remember your crimes and it fades with time. [b]Stolen goods[/b] are missed (0.21): seen, at once; unseen, half a day later. Push a town too far and it hires [b]bounty hunters[/b] who track you down.
[*][b]Quests and errands[/b] from townsfolk, and [b]alms and offerings[/b] that warm a faction towards you.
[*][b]Trophies and keepsakes[/b] in a new neck slot.
[*][b]Test features, off by default[/b] (search "test" in Settings): moving outlaw camps, harassment raids, marks on the world map, History saying why a faction turned, outpost history, prisoner cases and deals, Western Hive envoys, recruiting beaten rivals and more. Try them if you like; reports welcome.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements and install[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]MeitouClient 0.17.3 to 0.23 (0.23 recommended).[/b] Faces and squad roles need 0.19; stolen goods and the test features need 0.21; right-clicking keepsakes and warrants needs 0.20.2.
[*]Subscribe, then tick [b]LivingWasteland[/b] in the Meitou launcher, [b]below[/b] your other mods.
[*]New game recommended: in an existing save your record starts from zero.
[/list]

[h2]Plays well with[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Proving Grounds for Meitou:[/b] arena bouts are sport (no heat, no rivals from the ring); wins in the pit become news and get a victory line.
[*][b]Recap:[/b] titles, last stands, avenged squadmates, bounty hunters and more appear in its "Previously on".
[*][b]Rivalry Core[/b] (The Ashen Hand and others): one hostile party after you at a time, whichever mod sends it.
[*][b]Fiefdom[/b] and other town mods: their townspeople and guards stay out of your squad record, fights and rivals.
[*][b]Kenshi Ultimate Overhaul:[/b] its armies march for real and your lookouts know an army from a patrol; our small raids step aside.
[*][b]Guild & Quests[/b], [b]Raid Commander[/b], [b]Quest Log & Town Map[/b]: ranks, seals, beaten raids and our outlaws show up where they belong.
[*][b]Execution[/b], [b]Brutality[/b], [b]Meitou Transport[/b]: finishing blows, severed limbs, rides and voyages all count in the record.
[/list]

[h2]Reporting a bug[/h2]
Quit Meitou (before starting it again: the log is kept only until then), run [b]Save logs for a bug report.bat[/b] from the download (or grab log.txt from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Meitou\versions\<version>), and post the zip in the comments or on Discord with a few words and a screenshot.

[h2]Known in this version[/h2]
[list]
[*]The neck slot has no label yet (Meitou's slot API has none). It sits under the weight box; if it overlaps anything on your screen, move it with the two "Neck slot position" settings (restart after).
[*]A squad running at very high Athletics can outpace bounty crews: they walk.
[/list]
