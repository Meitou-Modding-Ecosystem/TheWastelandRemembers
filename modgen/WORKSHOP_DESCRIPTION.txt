[h1]The Wasteland Remembers[/h1]
[i]Towns talk, outlaws react, your squad keeps its own story.[/i]

The Wasteland Remembers is meant as [b]vanilla-plus[/b]: the Kenshi you know, with a world that notices you. No new super weapons, no new races, no rewritten factions. Just more life, more memory and more consequences, written to fit the lore.

[b]Nearly every part of this mod can be switched off.[/b] Open the [b]Squad record[/b] window (press [b]U[/b], or the button near the top right) and go to its [b]Settings[/b] tab: features have On/Off buttons (search them by typing, hover over one to see what it does), and a single slider sets how much of the added talk you hear, from all of it down to none. One click on [b]Record only[/b] turns everything off except your squad's record. Changes apply at once and are kept between sessions. (The same settings are also under Esc > Settings > Mods.)

[b]Early version.[/b] It has been play tested, but by few people so far: expect rough edges. [b]Feedback and bug reports are very welcome[/b]: tell me what you liked, what felt off and what broke (see [b]Reporting a bug[/b] below). Every report helps.

[h2]Your squad's story[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Squad record[/b] (press [b]U[/b]): kills by faction, knockouts, limbs taken and lost, damage, fights won, last stands, distance walked, ridden and sailed, crafting, and hours on every kind of work.
[*][b]75 titles[/b] from real deeds: the Hard Hand, Bonepicker, the Headsman, the Walker... Kill the Holy Lord Phoenix and you become [b]Narko[/b].
[*][b]Factions react to your titles[/b]: some admire, some fear, some despise and won't hire you. An admiring barman may give you a drink on the house.
[*][b]History[/b] and [b]the Fallen[/b]: who died, where, who killed them, whether they were avenged, and an epitaph. [b]Save as text[/b] keeps it all.
[*][b]Notices[/b] for skill levels and faction standing (+3 green, -3 red). Your people mourn the fallen by name, and grieve for days.
[/list]

[h2]A world that talks[/h2]
[list]
[*]Thousands of new lines, checked against the lore, for townsfolk, bosses and factions vanilla barely voices.
[*][b]Bar talk[/b], [b]town criers[/b] with the real news of your world, [b]lookouts[/b] who call out hostiles by name, and rumours about what actually happened: who you killed, which outlaw took a beating, which town is at war. News stays near where it happened.
[*][b]The Atlas[/b]: the places you know and the people talked about, filled by the gossip your squad really overhears. Every faction you've met, how it stands with you and its own title for you (from Heathen to The Devil). With Meitou 0.19, each person's page shows their face.
[/list]

[h2]Rivals: the ones who got away[/h2]
Win a fight and leave someone alive, and they may not let it go. The survivor, the actual person your squad beat, takes a new name for what you did to them: [i]Hollin One-Arm[/i], [i]Tesk Crutch[/i], [i]Mara the Unbowed[/i]. Once you've walked away, word reaches you that they crawled off and swore to come back.
[list]
[*]A day or two later they come [b]hunting your squad[/b], across the map, a little stronger each time. One who lost a leg comes back on an iron leg.
[*]Beat them and they may get away again. Lose, and they'll boast about it in every bar.
[*]Kill them or put them in chains to end it for good.
[*]Townsfolk near a rival talk about them by name, the Atlas keeps their story, and your History and Recap note every turn.
[/list]
With no rival about, your next won fight that leaves someone notable alive always makes one. With one about, now and then more, at most three at a time.

[h2]Outlaws and work[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]36 named outlaws[/b] who roam their own ground (when one falls, someone from their gang takes over a few days later under a new name, with a backstory and a bounty of their own), with contracts, bounties that grow while they raid, and pay that rises with the distance. Finished contracts warm the faction that posted them (up to friendly, never allied).
[*][b]Quests and errands[/b] from townsfolk, [b]alms and offerings[/b] for the Empire's poor, Okran's temple or the Anti-Slavers, and [b]town heat[/b]: towns remember your crimes, and it fades with time.
[*][b]Trophies and keepsakes[/b] in a new neck slot.
[*][b]Bounty hunters[/b]: make a town hunt you and it hires a crew to bring you in. They follow you across the map and attack. Beat them and one carries a warrant with your squad's name on it. Hurt a named outlaw's people and they may send men after you too.
[*][b]Squad roles[/b] (Meitou 0.19): give a squad a routine from the game's own NPC AI (guard your base, patrol, rest, wander) or have it [b]escort[/b] another squad. Your orders always come first.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements and install[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]MeitouClient 0.17.3 or later[/b] (0.19 or 0.20 recommended). Subscribe, then tick [b]LivingWasteland[/b] (the working name) in the Meitou launcher, below your other mods.
[*]Nearly every feature can be switched off in the Settings tab (press U). New game recommended.
[/list]

[h2]Reporting a bug[/h2]
Quit Meitou, then send [b]log.txt[/b] from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Meitou\versions\<your version> in the comments or on Discord, with a few words on what happened. (The log is replaced the next time Meitou starts.) Press F10 and pick "Wasteland Remembers: squad record" to see which build you have.

[h2]Known in this version[/h2]
[list]
[*]The neck slot has no label yet (Meitou's slot API has none).
[*]Moving outlaw camps and harassment raids are still in testing and [b]off by default[/b].
[*]Rivals, bounty hunters, outlaw reprisals and outlaw successors are new: on by default, and each can be switched off in Settings if anything looks wrong.
[/list]

[h2]Plays well with[/h2]
[b]Recap[/b] (our titles, last stands and more join its story), [b]Execution[/b] and [b]Brutality[/b] (every finishing blow and severed limb counts), [b]Guild & Quests[/b], [b]Raid Commander[/b], [b]Quest Log & Town Map[/b], [b]Meitou Transport[/b] and [b]Kenshi Ultimate Overhaul[/b] (its armies march for real; our raids step aside). With [b]Landmark Banners[/b] the Atlas lists what each town you visited has (a bar, a doctor, a police station). With [b]TimeLight Map[/b], both sit top right by default: move the map (console: topcam corner bottom-right) or our button (Settings > Squad record button position).
